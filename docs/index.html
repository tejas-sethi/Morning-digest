Can Australia make housing affordable while avoiding a major market crash? We may soon find out
The Guardian Australia
With the Reserve Bank hiking rates and property values in retreat, The Guardian examines whether Australia can thread the needle of gradual affordability improvement without triggering a crash. Uefa maintains World Cup boycott still stands despite Fifa's backing for Infantino
The Guardian Football
UEFA has reaffirmed its threat to boycott FIFA-run competitions, insisting that a FIFA vote backing Gianni Infantino's presidency 'changes nothing' in terms of its loss of confidence in his leadership. The global players' union Fifpro has piled on, condemning what it calls 'a profound abuse of presidential power,' while South American federations are also stirring. With the next World Cup cycle approaching, this governance crisis has the potential to reshape international football in ways that would affect every club — including United's Champions League ambitions.
These 3 charts show female-dominated jobs are actually the most exposed to AI
The Conversation AU
New analysis finds that 15 of the top 20 occupations most at risk of AI automation are predominantly held by women, upending the common assumption that it's mostly male-dominated technical or manual roles facing disruption. The data points to administrative, care-coordination, and clerical roles — many of which sit squarely in the NDIS and disability services sector — as particularly exposed. As you build out Lumen's team and systems, this is worth factoring into how you design roles and workflows from day one. This challenges the assumption that AI disruption is primarily a concern for tech or manufacturing industries — the disability support sector's administrative backbone may be more exposed than most people realise.
