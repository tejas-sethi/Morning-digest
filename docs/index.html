The Guardian Australia

The move ties Australia's AI infrastructure buildout directly to its renewables transition, potentially reshaping where and how tech investment lands domestically. This challenges the assumption that AI growth and energy policy are separate conversations — increasingly, where your data lives is an energy and policy decision as much as a technical one.
