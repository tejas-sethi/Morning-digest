Is it a gift or a loan when parents help kids buy a home? How to avoid family fights over money
The Conversation AU
Research finds that when parents chip in for a home purchase, the two parties often hold completely different mental models of whether it's a gift or a loan — and rarely have an explicit conversation about it, creating financial and relational risk down the track. The study recommends putting terms in writing regardless of how close the family is, and revisiting expectations as circumstances change. This challenges the assumption that keeping family money arrangements informal is the simpler or kinder option — the evidence suggests the opposite.
