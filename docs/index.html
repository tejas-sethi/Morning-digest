Housing market eased 'more than anticipated', RBA says
ABC News Business
The RBA governor flagged that the housing market has softened more than expected, while warning that a recent oil price spike is complicating the central bank's path on monetary policy. For prospective buyers, easing market conditions could create opportunities heading into the October–November purchase window, but rate decisions remain uncertain. Worth watching closely as auction clearance rates and credit conditions evolve over the next few weeks.
Mortgage offset account errors mean some Australians are owed thousands of dollars. Are you among them?
The Guardian Australia
ASIC's review found Australian banks have paid out $55 million in compensation over two years to home loan customers who unknowingly overpaid interest due to offset account failures. If you're planning to take out a home loan before November, this is a timely reminder to scrutinise how your offset account is set up and verify your bank's track record with these products. It's worth asking lenders directly about their offset compliance history before signing.
Ben Carroll to become Victorian premier after Jacinta Allan quits
SmartCompany
Jacinta Allan has resigned as Victorian Premier after losing caucus support, with Deputy Premier Ben Carroll set to take over and promising to 'do things differently.' The leadership change comes as Victoria grapples with cost-of-living pressures, a contested construction sector, and a state election on the horizon — all of which could influence housing policy and stamp duty settings relevant to Melbourne buyers. A promised royal commission into the construction sector may also have downstream effects on property supply timelines.
Football transfer rumours: Manchester United to sign Juve's Francisco Conceição?
The Guardian Football
Rumours link Manchester United and manager Michael Carrick with a move for Portugal winger Francisco Conceição from Juventus, valued at around £43m, with United also said to be in the mix for other targets as the window enters its final stretch. Note: this item is from The Guardian's transfer rumours column and has not been verified by Fabrizio Romano — treat with the usual caution until confirmed.
Fifa accused of 'selling the soul of football' over $20bn World Cup commercial rights sale plan
The Guardian Football
FIFA has announced plans to sell the commercial rights to its tournaments — including the World Cup — to private investors in a move that would raise billions but has triggered fierce backlash from UEFA, leading European clubs, and politicians who say it would fundamentally compromise football's governance. UEFA is threatening legal action, calling it an attempt to 'sell the soul of football,' while UK Prime Minister Andy Burnham has also weighed in critically. The story raises serious questions about who ultimately owns and controls the sport's most iconic assets. This challenges the assumption that football's governing bodies are primarily stewards of the game rather than commercial operators willing to financialise its crown jewels.
