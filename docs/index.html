Mind the Ever-Growing Gap: Premier League Clubs Have Spent £1.4bn This Summer Alone
The Guardian Football
English clubs have collectively splashed more than £1.4bn on new players this summer — roughly £300m more than the entire Bundesliga, La Liga, Ligue 1, and Serie A combined. Chelsea's £117m Morgan Rogers signing and Spurs' record outlays are emblematic of a league that is financially detaching itself from European rivals at pace. This challenges the assumption that financial fair play and UEFA regulations are meaningfully closing the competitive gap — if anything, the Premier League's domestic TV and commercial revenues are accelerating divergence, not slowing it.
